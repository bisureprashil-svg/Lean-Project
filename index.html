Twice-a-week prep (Sun & Wed)

  • Chicken: ~1 kg raw boneless thighs per batch. Marinate (yogurt, ginger-garlic, chilli, salt, garam masala), bake at 220 °C / 425 °F ~25 min or air-fry. Portion into 150 g lunch + 100 g dinner boxes.
  • Dal / chana: 200 g dry lentils or chickpeas → 4 portions. Add spinach, tomato, onion; tadka with 1 tsp ghee per portion.
  • Rice: cook basmati, weigh portions, refrigerate. Cooled & reheated rice raises blood sugar a little less.
  • Eggs: pre-chop spinach, onion and tomato for quick 5:10 am omelettes, or boil a dozen for grab-and-go days.
  • Veg: wash & chop broccoli, beans, carrots; steam lunch portions.

How to progress

  • Pick a weight you could lift 2 more reps with at the end of each set. Form first, always.
  • When you hit the top of the rep range on every set, move up next session (next dumbbell, or a harder push-up).
  • Rest 60–90 s between sets. Write your top sets in the Today → Notes box.
  • Equipment: adjustable dumbbells (up to ~25 kg each) or resistance bands. At a gym, do the same moves with machines or barbells.
  • Sore for 2–3 days in week 1 is normal. Sharp or joint pain is not — skip that move and tell your doctor if it persists.

Coach

Your weekly dietitian review, supplements, food rules and check-ups.

The rules that matter most

  1. Hit protein every day (whey makes this easy).
  2. Hit your weekly training goals (Train tab) — they start easy and build up.
  3. No alcohol — it goes straight to the liver.
  4. No sugary drinks, juice, sweets, bakery, fried snacks.
  5. Last food before 6 pm, then nothing but water / herbal tea.
  6. Sleep 8 h (your 8:30 pm → 5 am routine is already ideal).
Fasting? You already fast ~11 hours overnight (6 pm → 5 am) and eat in an early-day window — the style of time-restricted eating with the best evidence for blood sugar. Don't shorten it further or skip breakfast: protein spread across the day protects muscle, and you start work at 6 am. If you want more, finish dinner by 5:30 pm.

Your supplements

None of these burn fat — food, lifting and sleep do. These cover gaps. Take the bottles to your doctor or pharmacist once, so they can check the doses with your liver and blood work in mind.

Add these foods

  • Leafy greens: spinach, methi, kale — stir into dal or eggs.
  • More veg variety: cauliflower, bell peppers, zucchini, onions, mushrooms (all "free" — fill half the plate).
  • Fish 2×/week: salmon, sardines or mackerel (swap for chicken).
  • Chia seeds daily (soaked), and purslane as a vegetable in dal or salad if you can find it.
  • 1 whole fruit a day: apple, berries, guava, orange — swap for 1 roti or 75 g rice. Never juice.
  • Black coffee 2–3 cups before noon: coffee is linked to lower liver-disease risk. No sugar.
  • Low-fat cottage cheese / paneer as protein swaps.

Cut out

  • Alcohol, soda, juice, sweet chai/lassi, flavoured yogurt
  • Mithai, biscuits, bakery, white bread, namkeen, chips
  • Deep-fried food, processed meat
  • Refined sugar and syrups ("natural" sugars like honey/jaggery count too)

Blood work worth asking for

Low energy, low mood and belly fat at 29 can have medical causes that make fat loss harder. Ask the family doctor for:

  • HbA1c, fasting glucose & fasting insulin
  • Liver panel (ALT, AST, GGT) and a liver fibrosis check (FIB-4 / FibroScan)
  • Lipids (cholesterol, triglycerides)
  • TSH (thyroid), morning testosterone
  • Vitamin D, B12, ferritin
  • Snoring or daytime sleepiness? Ask about a sleep apnea test.

Recheck in ~3 months to see the liver and blood-sugar numbers improve.

Eating out / off-plan

  • Tandoori chicken or tikka (no sauce), dal, salad, 1 roti. Skip naan, biryani, butter/cream curries.
  • One off-plan meal a week is fine. Log it in notes and carry on — don't skip the next meal.
